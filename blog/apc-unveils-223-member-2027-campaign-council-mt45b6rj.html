APC UNVEILS 223-MEMBER 2027 CAMPAIGN COUNCIL

Sat, 22 Aug 2026 08:58 GMT • Credit: Punch • D-Ray Show Blog News

The All Progressives Congress has made public a comprehensive 223‑member council tasked with steering its 2027 presidential campaign. The list, issued by the Presidency on Saturday, expands on an earlier announcement that identified only the top officers.
President Bola Ahmed Tinubu will chair the council, while Vice President Kashim Shettima and APC National Chairman Professor Nentawe Yilwatda assume the roles of Vice Chairmen I and II respectively. Senator Abdulaziz Yari has been named Director‑General, and Imo State Governor Hope Uzodinma will serve as Secretary.
The expanded council introduces new directorates covering finance, extractive industries, education, manufacturing, micro‑small‑medium enterprises, support group coordination, faith‑based mobilisation, protocol, labour union relations, special duties, stakeholder mobilisation, community support, digital operations, grassroots engagement and welfare. Former governors Abdullahi Ganduje and Babatunde Fashola have been appointed to senior stakeholder mobilisation positions, while Ministers of State for Petroleum Resources Heineken Lokpobiri and Ekperikpe Ekpo serve as deputy directors in the extractive sector. The education sector is headed by Minister Tunji Alausa with Minister of State for Education Suwaiba Ahmad as his deputy.
By populating the council with a blend of senior politicians, former governors and sitting ministers, the APC appears to be building a nationwide network capable of rapid mobilisation and policy coordination. The inclusion of dedicated faith‑based mobilisation directors for Christian and Muslim communities underscores the party’s focus on Nigeria’s religious demographics, while the new finance and MSME directorates aim to tap economic concerns ahead of the election.
The council’s first task will be to launch coordinated outreach across the country, aligning local structures with the party’s national agenda. Observers note that the timing—well ahead of the 2027 election calendar—provides the APC with a strategic head start. What remains unclear are the specific fundraising targets, the operational budget for each directorate, and how the council will interact with state‑level party structures as the campaign gains momentum.
