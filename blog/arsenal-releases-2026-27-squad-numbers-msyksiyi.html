ARSENAL RELEASES 2026/27 SQUAD NUMBERS

Tue, 18 Aug 2026 11:24 GMT • Credit: Punch • D-Ray Show Blog News

Arsenal announced its official jersey numbers for the 2026‑27 Premier League season on Tuesday, publishing the details on the club’s website. The release comes after a wave of transfers that reshaped the squad, with several veteran players departing and new signings arriving.
Among the newcomers, Brazilian midfielder Bruno Guimaraes has been allocated the No 39 shirt, a number he has traditionally worn in tribute to his father’s taxi licence. Greek forward Christos Tzolis receives the No 17 jersey, previously associated with former Arsenal players Oleksandr Zinchenko, Alexis Sanchez and Emmanuel Petit. French goalkeeper Illan Meslier is set to wear No 30, a number last worn by Raheem Sterling in the 2024‑25 season.
The club noted that the exits of Jakub Kiwior, Christian Norgaard and Leandro Trossard have left the numbers 15, 16, 19 and 18 vacant. Additionally, numbers 25 through 28 have not been assigned for the past two seasons, reflecting the fluid nature of squad composition.
The full roster of numbers confirms the presence of key figures such as goalkeeper David Raya (1), defender William Saliba (2), midfielder Martin Ødegaard (8) and striker Gabriel Jesus (9). Young talents like Bukayo Saka (7) and Ethan Nwaneri (18) remain central to the club’s plans, while seasoned professionals including Kai Havertz (21) and Declan Rice (27) round out the squad.
Arsenal’s statement emphasized that the new numbering scheme honors the club’s history while accommodating fresh faces, hinting at a blend of continuity and renewal for the upcoming campaign. The team will debut the new kits when the Premier League kicks off in August, and fans can expect the updated merchandise to hit stores shortly thereafter.
What remains to be seen is how the assigned numbers will translate into on‑field roles and whether the squad’s composition will deliver the title‑challenging performances the club aims for this season.
