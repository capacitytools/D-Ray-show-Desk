Google Gives Nigerian Students Free AI

Thu, 20 Aug 2026 17:43 GMT • Credit: Punch • D-Ray Show Blog News

Google announced on Thursday that it will provide eligible Nigerian tertiary students with a free 12‑month subscription to its Gemini AI Plus service, a package valued at roughly ₦92,400 per user. The offer, dubbed the Gemini Student Offer, is aimed at students aged 18 and above who are enrolled in accredited universities, polytechnics or colleges of education. Those who participated in the program during the previous academic year may renew their access after confirming their student status.

The subscription unlocks a suite of advanced artificial‑intelligence tools through the Gemini app, including higher usage limits, Gemini Omni, Gemini Live for real‑time voice conversations, Deep Research for project work, custom practice quizzes, unlimited file uploads and 400 GB of cloud storage. Google says the initiative is part of a broader rollout across 27 Sub‑Saharan African countries, with Nigeria among the first to benefit.

Olumide Balogun, Google’s Director for West and East Africa, emphasized that the move is intended to remove financial obstacles and help Nigeria’s young, entrepreneurial population leapfrog into the AI era. He noted that the free year‑long access is the second consecutive year Google is offering the service at no cost, aiming to help students master complex subjects, build in‑demand digital skills and compete globally.

Google also highlighted that the program is designed to support learning rather than facilitate academic dishonesty. The Gemini platform’s Guided Learning mode acts as a tutor, offering hints and prompting questions instead of delivering direct answers. In a DeepMind‑conducted study involving 1,763 West African students, participants using Guided Learning recorded learning gains equivalent to between 1.2 and 1.7 years of progress within eight weeks.
